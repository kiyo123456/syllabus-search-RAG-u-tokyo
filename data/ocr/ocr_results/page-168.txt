Introduction to Positive Psychology: Science, Application and Cultural Patterns

This introductory course on positive psychology ams to explore how to live a better, more satisfying life. Research in positive
Psychology demonstrates that a significant percentage of well-being comes from our own mindset。 our chosen activities, and our
lifestyle behaviors. To deal with the challenges in real-world life。 we need to develop our internal resources to bring the best of
ourselves. Specific topics include a brief overview of the key theories, research methods, and findings in the fiecld of positive
psychology, the goals and principles of positive psychology, the biological, emotional, psychological, and social influence on positive
psychology, and the poltics and economics of positive psychology among others. The course also involves the practical application
of theory and research findings in the field of positive psychology. Additionally, happiness and well-being in relation to cultural
patterns will be briefly discussed. Students critically evaluate the societal myths related to well-being and life satisfaction and reflect
on their current personal life events to promote an understanding ofpositive psychology. Students will have the opportunity to engage
in a series of scientifically validated, well-being-enhancing activities, and reflect on the processes of their own positive changes.
