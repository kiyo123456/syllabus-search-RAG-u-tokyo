Fluency-Oriented Workshop (FLOW)

駐馬の ・要 The course aims to build students* confidence and ability to engage fluently and critically in an academic
discussion, to be aware of their weaknesses in holding a discusson in English, and to have the tools to improve
autonomously.

成          方法         Grades are based on active participation in class activities and on related assignments.
