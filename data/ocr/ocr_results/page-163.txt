Broadcast Media and Media Literacy: Navigating the Era of Misinformation and Deepfakes

The primary objective of this course is to enhance students' English communication skills, focusing on listening, speaking, and
writing. Students will refine their listenng skills by engaging with authentic English-language TV news。 improve their speaking
skills through discussions, and develop their writing skills by responding to the materials presented. Additionally, the course wil
help students navigate a broad range of topics and issues in broadcast media. The course also aims to foster students' critical thinking
abilities。 enabling them to analyze stories from both traditional and new media and assess ther credibility. Throughout the course。
students will have opportunities to explore strategies for navigating the challenges of misinformation and deepfakes in the digital
age.
