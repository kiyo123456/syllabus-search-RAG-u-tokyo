To address these questions the coursewill explore how the Australian continent became the focus of the British Empire's strategic imperial goals after the Americanrevolution, and subsequently a new experiment' in settler colonisation after the abolition of slavery. Students will learn about theimportance of land in the development of settler colonialism, why Australia was distinctive from Britain's larger settler empire in notforging treaties with Indigenous peoples, how Indigenous societies have co-existed with settler societies, and how different patternsof migration have shaped the “settler nation' over two centurics.The first half of the course will investigate late 18th and 19th century developments in the Australian settler colonies and considerhow those aligned with, or differed from, the development of other British settler nations. With reference to other parts of the BritishCommonwealth, the second half of the course will investigate developments in the Australian settler nation from the federation ofthe colonies in 1901 to the social transformations brought about by the civil rights era in the late 20th century.
